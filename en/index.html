About the project

Green energy in an industrial hub

“STROIPERLIT-RIGA” Ltd (SIA) is developing a 3 MW solar park on a 4.2 ha plot next to the Knauf factory in Salaspils parish. The project combines solar generation with battery storage, delivering a stable and predictable energy source.

Revenue model

Three complementary revenue streams

The solar + BESS combination generates income not only from produced energy, but also from energy trading and grid-balancing services.

☀️

Solar energy sales

Sale of electricity produced by the solar panels to the grid or, by agreement, to a direct consumer — a base revenue source.

🔋

Battery arbitrage

Storing energy during low-price hours and selling it during high-price hours, capturing market price spreads.

⚡

Grid balancing

Possible participation in balancing services, subject to market prequalification; income depends on market conditions and is not guaranteed.

Frequently asked questions

SES and BESS in brief

What is BESS?

BESS (Battery Energy Storage System) is a battery system that stores electricity and returns it to the grid when needed. In the Ļipiņas project it is 5 MWh capacity with 2.5 MW power.

What is SES?

SES (solar energy system) is a solar panel power plant that generates electricity from sunlight. In the Ļipiņas project it is 3 MW.

How does a solar + BESS park work?

Solar panels generate electricity that can be sold to the grid or stored in batteries. The BESS stores energy during low-price hours and releases it during high-price hours, and may also provide grid-balancing services.

Why combine solar panels with batteries?

The combination can generate income from three streams — energy sales, price arbitrage and grid balancing — diversifying revenue and reducing dependence on a single source.

Where is the Ļipiņas project located?

In Salaspils parish, Salaspils county, on two land plots — “Ļipiņas” and “Ļipiņas 1”.
